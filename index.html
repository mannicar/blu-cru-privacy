Blu Cru Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Blu Cru is a workforce management app operated by Mannicode, LLC ("Mannicode," "we," "us") that helps construction crews track assignments, attendance, and daily work logs. This policy describes what information Blu Cru collects, why, and how it is used.

Who uses Blu Cru

Blu Cru accounts are provisioned by an employer (an organization using Blu Cru), not created by the public. A supervisor or administrator adds an employee's name, phone number, and role to the system as part of onboarding.

Information we collect

  • Name and phone number. Your phone number is your login identifier. We never store passwords for employee accounts.
  • Organization and role. Which company you work for, your role (crew member or foreman), and your reporting supervisor.
  • Location, only at check-in and check-out. When you clock in or out of a job site, Blu Cru records your device's GPS location at that moment, to confirm attendance at the job site. Blu Cru does not continuously track your location, does not monitor your movement between check-in and check-out, and does not collect location data at any other time.
  • Work assignments and daily logs. Project assignments, check-in/check-out times, and the work-completed and delay/issue notes you submit for a shift.
  • Device push token. Used only to deliver app notifications (e.g., a new assignment or a reminder to clock out).

SMS login codes

To log in, you request a one-time passcode sent via SMS to your phone number. We do not store the code itself once it has been used or has expired — only a one-way hash used to verify it. Standard message and data rates from your carrier may apply.

What we don't collect

Blu Cru does not collect photos of people, continuous or background location data, browsing activity, contacts, or any data unrelated to verifying attendance and tracking assigned work.

How information is used

Information collected through Blu Cru is used solely to operate the app for your employer: verifying attendance, managing assignments, and maintaining shift records your employer is responsible for keeping.

Who can see your information

Your employer (the organization that added your account) can see your work records within Blu Cru. Organizations using Blu Cru cannot see each other's employees, projects, or records, even when working the same job site. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties except service providers who help us operate the app (for example, our SMS and push-notification providers), solely to deliver those services.

Data retention

We retain work records for as long as needed to serve your employer's business and recordkeeping needs. To request deletion or have questions about your data, contact us at the address below.

Children's privacy

Blu Cru is a workforce tool for employees and is not directed at or intended for use by children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above.
