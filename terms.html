Blu Cru Terms and Conditions

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Blu Cru is a workforce management app operated by Mannicode, LLC ("Mannicode," "we," "us") that helps construction crews track assignments, attendance, and daily work logs. These terms describe the SMS messaging program used for account login.

Program description

Blu Cru uses SMS solely to deliver one-time login passcodes ("OTP") to employees of organizations that use Blu Cru. Messages are transactional, not marketing, and are sent only when a user requests a login code by entering their phone number in the Blu Cru app and tapping "Send code."

Message frequency

Message frequency depends on how often you log in. A typical user receives one message per login attempt. No recurring or scheduled messages are sent.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Support

For help, reply HELP to any message, or contact us at engineering@manni.co.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving SMS login codes from Blu Cru. Because Blu Cru uses SMS as the login method, opting out of SMS means you will not be able to receive a login code by text; contact your supervisor or administrator for an alternative way to access your account.

Eligibility

Blu Cru accounts are provisioned by an employer for its employees; the messaging program is not open to the general public and is not directed at children.

If these terms change, we will update the "Last updated" date above.
